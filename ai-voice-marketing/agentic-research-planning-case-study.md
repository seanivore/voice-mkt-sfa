# Agentic Research and Planning Case Study 

## Research Haul 

**STEP 1:** Go all in on Perplexity. Have a ton of documents to play around with and find insights. 

1. Used the handful of "PRO" and "DEEP RESEARCH" prompts with Perplexity. 
2. Gathered methodology from Claude for drilling down from prompt to prompt. 
3. Target market spanned Socioeconomic demographics and geographic locations. 
4. Wanted strategy, best practices, variables, etc. specifically for AI Voice Telemarketing. 
5. Kept the focus on Home Services industry; electrical and plumbing. 
6. By the last few prompts I was just genuinely interested in the strategy. 

**STEP 2:** Use Agents to synthesize the research into a manageable document, different themes, concepts, etc. 

1. Generally speaking the flow starts with one that synthesizes 
2. Then another pulls out themes, concepts, whatever they identify as important or I saw 
3. Next puts that information into some kind of practical framework for the medium, aka voice telephony 
4. Then we have one that just iterates from that point to make actual content samples
5. Finally one reviews all research against all produced documents and content for accuracy 
6. Then the last one just implements feedback from the previous review and polishes it up 

**STEP 3:** Send out agents to research all about the tool, Bland AI 

**STEP 4:** Repeat step 2 until the research is exhausted. Go back to step 1 as needed, etc.

### "Persona" X Strategy

*Starting point:* The path I took in the research started because I saw "Persona" as a UI tool in BLAND AI. 

- And then not having done voice telemarketing before, I wanted to see what the best practices were. 
- This led to marketing strategies and different variables used in interactions. 
- That got medium specific as to how to manipulate voice for AI in Bland AI. 

*Consequential Goal:* Come up with archetypes that could be written into scripts based on the strategies. Something that could be used to illustrate the concepts in a practical way, enough that other agents could take it and write X number of scripts. Split testing would be the next step. Then probably start at research again because the agents make it so easy to be more on top of the best of the best then ever before. 

### Conversation Variables 

*Next Consideration:* There is so much more in the research to find, but seeing the statistics for success rates based on different variables that are introduced into a conversation was really fascinating. 

## Hella Cheap, Hella Fast

I basically worked from when we ended our call on Tuesday afternoon until now, Wednesday evening. So much of it is once and done. The rest is an enjoyable amount of creative work. 

### Cost 

`$ 4.61` for all research findings on:

- Best-practice strategy for AI voice telemarketing for
- Home services industry focused; electrical and plumbing 
- Persona archetypes for voice telephony
- Script writing guide for voice telephony
- Voice modulation techniques for Bland AI
- Script examples for each persona in each strategy
- Technical guide for using Bland AI

Maybe about `$ 10` spent while doing testing and adjusting what kind of output I wanted from the agents. 

### Time Running the Workflow 

`~6 hours` of time fully focused on copywriting for the prompts.

- 1-2 hours prompt writing for research and synthesis 
- 3-4 hours prompt writing for implementation 

### Time Building Agents 

`~12 hours` getting bugs out of new tools to handle file size. 

- No move development time until more tools are added 
- Planning on spending time planning logic for branching workflows

## Quality 

I still cannot get over the quality of the output from the agents. Insightful. AI is just so comprehensive and makes connections that humans literally can't which makes them forever beyond as creative as a human could be. 

The most time I spend on the prompts only increased the quality of the output. They're like little actors and you just set a scene. You should read some of the prompts in the code. Claude was a Hollywood movie star "using what I've learned from my acting career to better the quality of the character in the AI Voice scripts." No joke. Insane. 

## Overview 

So there is a lot more value to be found and the bulk of the hard work is done. And then of course the ideas going forward are endless. I had one of the agents pull all the API information from Bland AI because imagine this: 

- Agents find topical conversation information 
- Agents keep all strategy up to date and adjust based on testing 
- Agents write endless scripts 
- The same agents are put in workflows that work with the features in the tool, making the calls, gathering the information on leads, etc. 
- Ever looking for next steps you just have one of them analyze everything and come up with best next steps for X goal
