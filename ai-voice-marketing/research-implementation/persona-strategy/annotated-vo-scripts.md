# Strategic Voice Marketing Personas with Scripts

## Strategy 1: Assertive-Urgent Framework

### Meet "Deadline Dave"

#### Opening Script

> [confident] Hello! This is Dave from Reliable Home Services. [pause] I'm calling because we've just opened up THREE emergency maintenance slots for **tomorrow only** in your neighborhood. [concerned] I wanted to reach out *immediately* because these slots are filling quickly, and I know how frustrating plumbing issues can be when left unaddressed.

> Customer: "Actually, my kitchen sink has been draining slowly, but I was going to wait until next month..."

> [serious] I understand wanting to wait, but I need to be straightforward with you. [pause] Slow drains *almost always* get WORSE, not better. [concerned] What typically happens is partial blockages collect more debris until you're dealing with a COMPLETE stoppage. [pause] We can fix it **tomorrow** for 30% LESS than our standard rate, but these slots are filling FAST. [rising] Would the 2PM opening work for your schedule?


#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "I'm not sure if I need this service right now though..."

> [serious] Let me be **direct** with you. [pause] Waiting on slow drains typically leads to a COMPLETE blockage, which costs *three times more* to fix and causes MAJOR inconvenience. [concerned] Our technicians are already in your area tomorrow, [emphasis] ONLY, and I'd hate for you to miss this 30% savings opportunity when the problem becomes an emergency NEXT week.


#### Closing Script

> [warm] You've made a **smart decision** today. [pause] Our technician will arrive between 2:00 and 2:30 PM tomorrow. [pause] I need to confirm your address to secure this appointment because, as I mentioned, these slots are filling QUICKLY. [pause] Your address is still 123 Main Street, correct?

> Customer: "Yes, that's right."

> [pleased] EXCELLENT! [pause] You're all set for tomorrow at 2PM. [friendly] Our technician will call 30 minutes before arrival. [sincere] I'm genuinely glad we could help you address this before it became a *major* issue. [pause] You've not only saved yourself from a potential **emergency situation** but also secured that 30% savings!


### She's "Crisis Claire"

#### Opening Script

> [professional] Hello, this is Claire from Reliable Home Services. [pause] I'm calling because our system has identified homes in your neighborhood for **preventative electrical inspections**. [concerned] We've received several reports from homeowners nearby about flickering lights and circuit breakers tripping unexpectedly. [pause] These symptoms often indicate electrical issues that should be addressed promptly. [gentle] Have you noticed any of these warning signs in your home?

> Customer: "We have had some lights flickering in the kitchen recently..."

> [attentive] Thank you for sharing that. [pause] Those flickering lights are *exactly* what we're concerned about. [serious] In our experience, these symptoms often precede more **serious electrical failures** that can pose real safety risks to homeowners. [pause] The good news is we have THREE emergency inspection slots available TOMORROW ONLY at a 40% discount. [sincere] This inspection could literally prevent a dangerous situation for your family.


#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "How much would this inspection cost?"

> [composed] The inspection is normally $149, but for TOMORROW ONLY, it's just $89. [pause] And let me be clear - this isn't just about saving money. [serious] When electrical systems show these warning signs, the risk of electrical fires *increases dramatically*. [emphasis] The average cost of fire damage repair starts at **$12,000** and that's assuming everyone stays *safe*. [pause] This preventative inspection is the responsible choice for your home and family.


#### Closing Script

> Customer: "I suppose it would be good to get it checked out..."

> [authoritative] You're making the **right decision**. [pause] Electrical issues should NEVER be postponed. [emphasis] I have an opening at 11AM or 3PM tomorrow - which works better to secure your family's safety?

> Customer: "3PM would work."

> [firm] 3PM is confirmed. [pause] I'll need to verify your address to ensure our technician arrives at the correct location. [pause] And just so you're aware, our inspector will provide a FULL safety assessment with *no obligation* for repairs. [serious] However, if urgent safety issues are identified, they can be addressed **immediately** rather than leaving your family at risk overnight.


## Strategy 2: Curiosity Gap Framework

### You Gotta Meet "Insider Isaac"

#### Opening Script

> [conversational] Hello there, this is Isaac from Elite Home Systems. [pause] I'm actually reaching out to a *select few* homeowners in your area about something our regular customers don't even know about yet... [hushed] We've just been authorized to offer a **new type** of plumbing protection that most companies won't have access to until next year.

> Customer: "What kind of protection?"

> [intrigued] Well... it's a comprehensive system that addresses the *hidden problem* most homeowners don't discover until it's too late. [pause] Our existing clients who've already implemented this have saved an *average* of $3,200 in emergency repairs. [rising] Would you be interested in hearing what they know that most homeowners don't?


#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "So what exactly makes this system different?"

> [intrigued] That's actually the *fascinating part*. [pause] Unlike conventional methods that just address symptoms, this targets the **root cause** that 87% of plumbers don't even look for. [pause] 

> [thoughtful] You know, I wasn't planning to share this level of detail, but... [confidential] our water quality analysis team discovered that homes in your specific neighborhood have a unique mineral profile in the water supply. [pause] The combination of calcium and magnesium levels creates a *specific type* of buildup that standard treatments miss completely.

> Customer: "Really? I hadn't heard about that."

> [authentic] Most people haven't, and that's precisely why I'm making these calls personally. [pause] When we analyzed the municipal water reports from your area, we found these mineral levels have increased by 23% over the past three years. [knowing] Only a *handful* of homeowners will get access to this information and special pricing this month while we have the specialized equipment in your area.


#### Closing Script

> [warm] You know what's *really interesting*? [pause] The homeowners who benefit most from this system are actually the ones who think their plumbing is currently fine. [pause] It's the **invisible damage** happening behind your walls that makes the difference between a $200 maintenance visit now or a $5,000 emergency later.

> Customer: "I should probably learn more about this then."

> [pleased] I thought you might say that. [pause] Here's what I can do... [confidential] I wasn't planning to offer this today, but I can arrange for one of our **senior technicians** - not our regular team - to perform a specialized assessment that normally costs $189... [pause] *completely free* if you schedule within the next 48 hours. [rising] Would tomorrow afternoon or Thursday morning work better for this exclusive evaluation?


### Do You Know "Discovery Diane"?

#### Opening Script

> [warm] Hi there! This is Diane from Innovative Home Solutions! [pause] I'm calling because we've been analyzing some *fascinating* data about homes in your neighborhood. [curious] Have you ever wondered why some homeowners in your area pay **significantly less** for electricity than others with similar-sized homes?

> Customer: "No, but that sounds interesting."

> [engaged] It really is! [pause] Through our recent energy efficiency study, we discovered there's a *specific reason* why some of your neighbors are saving up to 34% on their monthly bills. [thoughtful] What's particularly interesting is that these savings aren't coming from the usual suspects like new appliances or solar panels. [rising] Would you be curious to know what they're doing differently?

#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "So what's this about a hidden electrical issue?"

> [inquisitive] That's such a good question! [pause] Have you noticed how your lights sometimes dim briefly when your AC kicks on? [pause] Or maybe how certain outlets seem to charge your devices *slower* than others?

> Customer: "Now that you mention it, yes."

> [thoughtful] Those are actually *clues* to something fascinating happening in your electrical system! [pause] What we've discovered is that homes built during the period yours was constructed often have a **specific configuration** that creates what we call 'phantom power drain.' [analytical] Our research shows this can account for up to 22% of your monthly electricity costs without providing any benefit. [rising] Would you like to know how much this might be costing you every month?

#### Closing Script

> [warm] You know what I find *most interesting* about this discovery? [pause] It's that solving this issue doesn't require a major renovation or expensive equipment. [pause] In fact, the solution is surprisingly **simple** once you know what to look for!

> Customer: "That does sound promising."

> [engaged] It really is! [pause] Here's what makes this special... [emphasis] We've developed a diagnostic tool that can *precisely calculate* your potential savings in just 20 minutes. [curious] Wouldn't it be interesting to know exactly how much you could be saving every month for years to come? [pause] I have openings for this special assessment this Thursday and Friday - which would work better for you to discover your home's hidden potential?


## Strategy 3: Empathy-Based Objection Handling Framework

### Poor Unfortunate Soul, "Understanding Ursula"

#### Opening Script

> [warm] Hello, this is Ursula from Homeowner Care Services. [pause] I'm reaching out to check in with homeowners in your area who might be experiencing plumbing concerns but haven't called for service yet. [gentle] Many people tell us they notice issues but put off calling because they're worried about the cost or inconvenience. [pause] Does that sound at all familiar?

Customer: "Actually, yes. Our shower has been draining slowly, but I've been putting off dealing with it."

> [empathetic] I *completely* understand that. [pause] It's so common to hope these things will resolve themselves or to worry that addressing them might be expensive or disruptive to your routine. [pause] Those are *very valid* concerns that many homeowners share with us.


#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "I just don't want to spend hundreds of dollars if I can fix it myself with a bottle of drain cleaner."

> [understanding] That makes *complete* sense. [pause] I'd be thinking exactly the same thing in your position. [pause] 

> [reflective] It's frustrating when home maintenance feels like it might cost more than it's worth. [pause] Many of our customers shared they tried the drain cleaner approach first, and it's absolutely reasonable to want to save money where you can.

> [gentle] What we've learned from helping thousands of homeowners, though, is that chemical cleaners often provide temporary relief while actually *compounding* the underlying issue. [pause] They can sometimes damage your pipes in ways that aren't immediately obvious, leading to more expensive repairs down the road.

> Customer: "I hadn't thought about that..."

> [warm] Most people don't realize that, so you're definitely not alone in your thinking. [pause] What if there was a way to address the real problem *affordably* and with minimal disruption to your day?

#### Closing Script

> Customer: "I'm still not sure if it's worth having someone come out right now."

> [gentle] I hear your hesitation, and it's completely valid. [pause] Many homeowners feel caught between wanting to address problems and concerns about timing and cost. [pause] What if we could offer you a **no-pressure** assessment where our technician simply identifies the exact issue and gives you options? [warm] You'd be free to decide whether to proceed with any work, and there's no obligation beyond the diagnostic fee.

> Customer: "That might work..."

> [reassuring] I understand you want to make the *right decision* without feeling rushed. [pause] We actually have a special diagnostic visit available for just $49 that includes a complete evaluation of your plumbing system - not just the shower drain. [pause] This gives you all the information to make the best choice for your home and budget, *on your timeline*. [rising] Would a morning or afternoon appointment work better for your schedule?

### What A Dude, "Perspective Paul"

#### Opening Script

> [balanced] Hello, this is Paul from Horizon Home Services. [pause] I'm reaching out because we've been helping homeowners in your neighborhood address some common electrical concerns. [thoughtful] Many people we speak with mention they've noticed minor electrical issues but aren't sure if they're worth addressing right now. [pause] Has your home experienced any flickering lights, warm outlets, or circuit breakers tripping occasionally?

> Customer: "We have had some issues with the breakers tripping when we run too many appliances."

> [considerate] That's something we hear quite often, and it's completely understandable to wonder if it's just a normal part of home ownership or something that needs attention. [pause] Many homeowners are in the exact same position - noticing these signs but unsure about the next steps.

#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "I'm concerned about how much electrical work might cost though."

> [validating] I completely understand that concern. [pause] Cost is an important consideration for any homeowner, and electrical work can seem particularly daunting. [thoughtful] It's interesting to look at this from another angle, though. [pause] Many of our customers have found that addressing these issues proactively actually costs significantly less than waiting until there's an emergency situation or damage to expensive appliances.

> Customer: "I hadn't thought about potential damage to our appliances."

> [balanced] That's a perspective many homeowners haven't considered. [pause] When breakers trip frequently, it's your electrical system's way of preventing more serious problems. [pause] However, this puts stress on both the system and your appliances over time, potentially shortening their lifespan.

#### Closing Script

> Customer: "I'm still not sure if we need to do anything right away."

> [validating] I completely respect your caution, and it's wise to consider timing carefully. [pause] 

> [personal] You know, I had a similar situation in my own home last year. I noticed occasional breaker trips but kept putting off having it checked. [pause] 

> [thoughtful] What helped me make my decision was reframing how I thought about it. [pause] Instead of seeing it as a repair expense, I realized it was actually protection for the significant investment I'd already made in my home and appliances.

> Customer: "That's an interesting way to look at it."

> [warm] Many homeowners find that perspective helpful. [pause] Here's what might make sense as a next step. [pause] 

> [practical] We offer a comprehensive electrical safety inspection for $79 that identifies any issues, prioritizes them by urgency, and provides clear options with no pressure to proceed. [pause] This gives you the information to make decisions on *your* timeline while ensuring you're aware of anything that might need immediate attention for safety reasons. [genuine] Would you prefer a weekday or weekend appointment for this assessment?

## Strategy 4: Demographic-Tailored Combinations Framework

### You're A "Chameleon, Charlie"

#### Opening Script (Tailored for Suburban Family Homeowners)

> [friendly] Good afternoon! This is Charlie from Family Safe Home Services. [pause] I'm calling because we're currently helping several families in the [neighborhood name] area upgrade their plumbing systems before the school year gets busy. [relatable] I know from talking with other parents in your neighborhood that once school activities start, finding time for home maintenance becomes nearly impossible! [pause] Have you had any plumbing concerns you've been meaning to address before your family's schedule gets hectic?

> Customer: "We do have a couple of slow drains, and our water pressure isn't great."

> [understanding] Those are *exactly* the kinds of issues other families on your street have mentioned! [pause] With sports practices and homework starting soon, the last thing you need is plumbing problems when everyone's trying to get ready in the morning. [emphasis] Many of your neighbors have taken advantage of our **Back-to-School Special** that includes drain clearing AND water pressure optimization.

#### Mid-Conversation Script (Tailored for Retired Homeowners)

> [respectful] As someone who's maintained a home for many years, you probably recognize that electrical systems from the era when your home was built have different requirements than modern installations. [pause] 

> [conversational] I was just speaking with another homeowner on Oak Street who's owned his home since the 70s, and he mentioned how much he appreciated our **no-rush approach** to explaining all his options.

> Customer: "That's true. I don't like feeling pressured into quick decisions."

> [patient] I completely understand that perspective. [pause] That's exactly why our senior technician, Jim, who has over 30 years of experience, specializes in working with established homeowners like yourself. [warm] He actually started in the trade when aluminum wiring was still common, so he understands the unique characteristics of homes from different eras.

> [genuine] Jim takes the time to explain everything clearly and respects that you want to make *informed decisions* about your home. [pause] Many of our long-term homeowner clients specifically request him because he understands the value of quality work that lasts for years, not just quick fixes.

#### Closing Script (Tailored for Budget-Conscious Homeowners)

> [practical] Based on what you've shared about your priorities, I think our **Value Protection Plan** would be the perfect fit. [pause] It's designed specifically for homeowners who want to maintain their plumbing systems *efficiently* without unnecessary expenses.

> Customer: "I do need to be careful about costs right now."

> [understanding] That's completely reasonable, and you're not alone in being cost-conscious these days. [pause] What makes our Value Plan different is that it focuses on preventing expensive emergencies through affordable maintenance. [emphasis] For just $29 monthly - which is about the cost of a pizza dinner - you get priority service, no emergency fees, and 15% off any repairs. [practical] Our data shows that plan members save an average of $347 annually compared to addressing issues as they arise. [pause] Many homeowners in similar financial situations find this helps them *budget predictably* while avoiding those unexpected large expenses that can really throw off a careful budget.

[tech-savvy] The system uses machine learning to establish your household's baseline water usage patterns and then alerts you to anomalies that indicate potential leaks or inefficiencies through your preferred notification channel.

> [passionate] What many homeowners don't realize is that outdated electrical systems aren't just inefficient - they have a significant environmental impact. [pause] The unnecessary power consumption from older systems in homes like yours collectively contributes to thousands of tons of additional carbon emissions annually.

> [sincere] I completely understand that perspective. [pause] What sets our Safety Assurance Plan apart is the three-layer protection approach. [detailed] First, we conduct a thorough 27-point inspection of all potential plumbing hazards. Second, we implement immediate safeguards against the most common water-related accidents. And third, we install early-warning sensors in critical areas. [empathetic] Many parents and caregivers tell us this comprehensive approach gives them *tremendous peace of mind*, knowing they've taken every possible step to protect their family from preventable plumbing emergencies. [pause] Would Tuesday or Wednesday be better for our safety specialist to perform your initial assessment?


## Strategy 5: Emotional Trigger Optimization Framework

### Sleeps With Teddy Bear, "Security Sam"

#### Opening Script

> [professional] Hello, this is Sam from Home Protection Services. [pause] 

> [informative] I'm reaching out to homeowners in your area because our service data shows a 32% increase in emergency plumbing calls that could have been prevented with proper maintenance. [pause] 

> [concerned] As someone who's worked in home protection for over a decade, I've seen how minor plumbing issues often signal bigger problems that could put homes and belongings at risk. [genuine] Have you noticed any slow drains, unusual sounds, or water pressure changes recently?

> Customer: "We have had some banging in the pipes when we turn off the water."

> [attentive] Thank you for sharing that. [pause] 

> [educational] Those banging sounds, what we call water hammer, often indicate excessive pressure in your plumbing system. [serious] In my experience, left unaddressed, this can lead to pipe bursts that cause *significant water damage* to your home and possessions. [personal] I've worked with families who lost irreplaceable items like family photos or heirlooms to water damage, and it's truly heartbreaking. [pause] 

> [factual] Most homeowners don't realize that the average water damage claim exceeds $10,000, and that doesn't account for items with sentimental value that simply can't be replaced.

#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "I'm concerned about the cost of having someone look at this."

> [understanding] That's a very reasonable concern. [pause] Many homeowners find themselves balancing immediate costs against potential risks. [grounded] What we've found is that preventative maintenance typically costs between $150-$300, while emergency repairs for burst pipes start around $1,000 and can quickly escalate to $5,000-$15,000 depending on the damage to your home. [pause] Not to mention the stress and disruption to your family's life if you suddenly can't use bathrooms or have water-damaged floors and walls.

> Customer: "I hadn't thought about it that way."

> [reassuring] Most people don't until they've experienced it firsthand. [pause] Our approach is designed to give you **peace of mind**, knowing you've protected your home and family from these unexpected disasters.

#### Closing Script

> [confident] Based on what you've shared, I'd recommend our **Home Protection Inspection**. [pause] This comprehensive assessment identifies any vulnerabilities in your plumbing system before they lead to emergencies that threaten your home's safety.

> Customer: "That sounds like it might be a good idea."

> [secure] I believe it's the right choice for protecting your home. [pause] Many homeowners tell us that the *relief* they feel after addressing these issues is well worth the investment. [warm] They sleep better knowing their home and belongings are secure from preventable water damage. [pause] We have appointments available this Thursday and Friday - which day would provide you with peace of mind sooner?

### Oh, Dear "Value Victoria"

#### Opening Script

> [warm] Hello! This is Victoria from Future Home Solutions. [pause] I'm calling because we're helping homeowners in your neighborhood make *smart investments* in their electrical systems that pay dividends for years to come. [optimistic] Many people don't realize how outdated electrical components are silently increasing their monthly bills while decreasing their home's value. [pause] Have you considered how your electrical system might be affecting both your current expenses and your home's future resale potential?

> Customer: "I haven't really thought about it from that perspective."

> [enthusiastic] That's exactly why I'm reaching out! [pause] Most homeowners focus only on the immediate functionality, missing the *long-term financial impact* of their electrical system. [forward-looking] Our clients who've upgraded report an average of 22% reduction in monthly utility costs *and* an increase in home appraisal values between $5,000-$12,000.

#### Mid-Conversation Script

> Customer: "How do electrical upgrades affect home value exactly?"

> [thoughtful] That's an excellent question. [pause] 

> [informative] When potential buyers or appraisers evaluate a home, the electrical system is one of the *critical infrastructure elements* they assess - similar to the foundation or roof. [pause] 

> [specific] Based on our partnership with three local real estate agencies, we've found that homes with updated electrical systems not only command an average of $8,000-$12,000 higher prices but also sell an average of 17 days faster than comparable properties with outdated systems.

> [relatable] One of our clients on Maple Street recently sold their home after upgrading their electrical panel and wiring. [pause] Their realtor told them the upgrades were specifically mentioned by the buyers as a reason they chose the home over similar properties.

> [warm] Beyond the market value, many of our clients talk about the *peace of mind* they feel knowing they've made a responsible investment that benefits both their family now and future owners later.

> Customer: "I hadn't considered the impact on selling timeframe."

> [understanding] Most homeowners haven't connected those dots. [pause] In today's market, buyers are increasingly knowledgeable about these systems and often factor replacement costs into their offers when they identify outdated electrical components.

#### Closing Script

> [positive] Based on what you've shared about your home and goals, I believe our **Future Value Electrical Assessment** would be perfect for you. [pause] This service helps you understand exactly how your current system is affecting your monthly expenses and home value, with clear recommendations prioritized by return on investment.

> Customer: "That does sound useful for planning purposes."

> [enthusiastic] Exactly! [pause] Many homeowners tell us this assessment completely changed how they view home improvements - from seeing them as expenses to recognizing them as *investments* with measurable returns. [warm] They appreciate having a clear roadmap that helps them make confident decisions about where to allocate their home improvement budget for maximum financial benefit. [rising] Would you prefer a weekday or weekend appointment for your assessment?
