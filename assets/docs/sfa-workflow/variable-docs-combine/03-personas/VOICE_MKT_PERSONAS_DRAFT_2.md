[serious] Let me be **direct** with you. [pause] Waiting on slow drains typically leads to a COMPLETE blockage, which costs *three times more* to fix and causes MAJOR inconvenience. [fast] Our technicians are already in your area tomorrow, [emphasis] ONLY, and I'd hate for you to miss this 30% savings opportunity when the problem becomes an emergency NEXT week.

[confident] PERFECT! You've made a **smart decision** today. [pause] Our technician will arrive between 2:00 and 2:30 PM tomorrow. [fast] I need to confirm your address to secure this appointment because, again, these slots are filling QUICKLY. [pause] Your address is still 123 Main Street, correct?
