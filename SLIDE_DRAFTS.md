## Strategy: Turning Uncertainty into Action
**When fear freezes action, homes suffer in silence**
Example Script:
```
[urgent][higher pitch] Your neighbors called about water damage spreading through their walls.
[pause 0.8s][lower pitch] They waited too long... and insurance denied their claim.
[empathetic][slower] I'm calling because we've spotted the same warning signs in your area.
[emphasize: protected] Let me get you protected now, before your home tells the same story.
```
--> 28% increase in same-day scheduling
--> 42% reduction in "let me think about it" responses

## Strategy: Making Financial Decisions Feel Safe
